# SOP: Responding to customer reviews & complaints

## Principles

1. **Never suppress, hide or delay a negative review.** Never ask a customer to change a rating in exchange for anything (compliance/ftc-endorsement-guides).
2. Respond to every 1-2★ review on owned channels within **2 business days**.
3. Respond in brand voice (brand/voice-guide): warm, honest, no blame.
4. **No medical advice.** Do not diagnose. Do not tell the customer a reaction is "just purging" or "normal".
5. Replies are customer-facing copy, so brand/claims-policy applies.

## Triage

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Texture, scent, packaging, price or "didn't work for me" | Reply with the template below. Offer usage tips. Mention the return policy only if the order is within policy. |
| Breakouts / "purging" | Reply with the breakout template. Suggest stopping use and a patch test before reintroducing. No promises. |
| Burning, stinging, rash, swelling, or anything that needed a doctor | **Stop.** Follow sop/adverse-event-escalation before replying. |
| Wrong item, damaged or missing package | Create a care ticket. The return policy does not cover damaged items, so escalate to a Care lead. |
| Request for a refund outside 15 days, or an international order | Explain the policy (policy/returns) and escalate to a Care lead for a goodwill decision. Do not promise an exception. |

## Reply templates

**Generic "didn't work for me"**

> Hi {name}, thank you for sharing this, and we're sorry {product} wasn't the right match. {one usage tip relevant to the complaint}. If you ordered from glowrecipe.com within the last 15 days, email returns@glowrecipe.com and we'll send a free return label. 💧

**Pilling under makeup** (top complaint for P481989 sunscreen and present for P479327 moisturizer)

> Hi {name}, thanks for the honest feedback. Pilling can happen when layers don't have time to set. Many in our community wait 1-2 minutes before makeup and use a lighter layer underneath. We've shared your note with our product team.

**Breakouts**

> Hi {name}, we're sorry to hear that. Everyone's skin is different. We recommend pausing use and doing a patch test before trying again. If you have any discomfort, please reach out to care@... so we can help directly.

## Usage tips by complaint theme

These tips draw on what reviewers themselves report in research/insights-*. They are guidance, not claims.

- **Sticky feel** (Dew Drops P466123, Pink Juice P428819): use less product and let it absorb before the next layer.
- **Scent** (Pink Juice P428819, Blueberry P436359): be honest that the product is fragranced. Do not offer a "fragrance-free version", because none exists in the catalog.
- **Pilling** (Sunscreen P481989): see the template above.
