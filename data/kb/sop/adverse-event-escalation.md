# SOP: Adverse reaction escalation

## Trigger

Use this SOP when a customer tells us **directly**, by email, chat, DM or phone, that they had a reaction to a product. Reactions include burning, stinging, rash, swelling, blistering, eye irritation, or anything they saw a doctor for.

Public reviews on retailer sites are monitored separately (sop/review-response). If a public review describes a severe reaction, invite the customer to contact Care directly.

## Steps

1. **Do not give medical advice.** Tell the customer to stop using the product and to seek medical care if symptoms are severe or getting worse.
2. **Collect within the same conversation:**
   - product name and product ID
   - batch or lot code from the pack
   - purchase channel (glowrecipe.com, Sephora, other)
   - date of first use and date of reaction
   - description of the reaction, and whether medical treatment was needed
   - photos, if the customer offers them
   - other products used at the same time
3. **Classify:**

| Level | Definition | Action | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Serious** | Matches the MoCRA definition: serious or persistent rash, 2nd/3rd-degree burn, significant hair loss, persistent change in appearance, or medical/surgical intervention needed | Escalate to Regulatory **the same business day**. Regulatory files with FDA (MedWatch 3500A). | FDA report within **15 business days** of receipt |
| **Non-serious** | Mild or temporary stinging, redness or breakouts with no medical care | Log in the reaction tracker. Offer return or refund per policy/returns, or a goodwill gesture. | Log within 1 business day |
| **Unclear** | Not enough information | Ask follow-up questions and treat as **Serious** until classified | same business day |

4. **Keep records** of all reports, serious or not.
5. **Follow up.** If new medical information arrives within 1 year of a serious report, send it to Regulatory. It must reach FDA within 15 business days.

## Customer script

> I'm so sorry this happened. Please stop using {product} and, if your skin is still uncomfortable, see a healthcare professional. To help our safety team look into it, could you share the batch code on the bottom of the pack, when you started using it, and what you experienced? We take every report seriously.

## Do not

- Do not say "this is normal", "it's just purging" or "no one else has had this".
- Do not ask the customer to delete or edit a review.
- Do not promise compensation in exchange for not reporting.
