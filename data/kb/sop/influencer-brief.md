# SOP: Writing an influencer / creator brief

## When to use

Use this SOP for every paid, gifted or affiliate creator collaboration. Gifted PR boxes count too. Under the FTC Endorsement Guides, free product is a material connection (compliance/ftc-endorsement-guides).

## Steps

1. **Pick the product and angle.** Start from research/insights-<product_id>. Use a theme that customers actually praise (Leans = praise). Do not build the angle on a theme that mostly appears in complaints.
2. **Fill in creative/brief-template.**
3. **Add the product's mandatory rules** from the quick-lookup table in brand/claims-policy.
4. **Regulatory pre-approval** is required when the product has CP-02, CP-04, CP-05 or CP-08 rules, which cover BHA, AHA, sunscreen and retinol (sop/ad-claim-review).
5. **Send the brief, then review the draft post** before it goes live. Check disclosure, claims, and that the creator describes only their own experience.
6. **Monitor after posting.** Spot-check posts within 48 hours. If the disclosure is missing or a claim is blocked, ask for an edit within 24 hours. If the creator does not fix it, end the collaboration.

## Mandatory brief sections

- **Disclosure (CP-10):** "#ad" or "Ad" at the start of the caption **and** on screen in the first 3 seconds of video. "#gifted" alone is not acceptable. Repeat the disclosure in every post.
- **Honest experience:** the creator must use the product for at least 14 days before posting. They may mention downsides.
- **Do not say** (product-specific): copy from brand/claims-policy.
- **Must say** (product-specific): for example "reapply every 2 hours" for SPF, or "use SPF daily" for AHA and retinol products.
