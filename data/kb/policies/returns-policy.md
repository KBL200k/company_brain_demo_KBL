# Returns and refund policy

Summary of the public policy on glowrecipe.com, as retrieved on 2026-10-06. This covers orders placed on **glowrecipe.com**. Products bought at Sephora follow Sephora's own return policy, which is **not** in this knowledge base.

## Key terms

- **Return window:** 15 days from the purchase date.
- **How to start a return:** "E-mail returns@glowrecipe.com to receive a complimentary return label." Return shipping is free with this label.
- **Refund method:** "A credit will be automatically applied to your original method of payment." The card issuer may take an additional 3–7 business days to post it.
- **Not refunded:** original shipping and handling charges.

## Exclusions

- One-time-use products, such as sheet masks.
- International orders and orders shipped to Canada.
- Lost or stolen packages ("We are not responsible for lost or stolen packages").
- Customs duties or fees.

## Not covered by the policy text

The public policy does **not** say anything about:

- exchanges
- damaged or defective items on arrival
- returns of opened or used products. The policy does not say whether a product must be unused.

For these cases, the assistant should say the policy does not cover it and point to sop/review-response (customer care escalation). It should not guess.

Brand statement from the policy page: "We absolutely want you to be 100% satisfied with your purchase."
