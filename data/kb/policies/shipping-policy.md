# Shipping policy

Summary of what glowrecipe.com published on its shipping policy page as of 2026-10-06.

## What the page states

- **Free U.S. shipping on orders of $40 or more.** The banner reads "FREE U.S. SHIPPING • With Orders $40+".
- Signing up for email gives **free shipping on the first order**.
- Signing up for SMS also qualifies for free shipping, plus early access to launches.

## What the page does NOT state

The retrieved page has **no details** on:

- order processing time
- shipping methods or delivery times
- shipping cost for orders under $40
- international shipping destinations
- order tracking
- what happens with lost or damaged packages. The returns policy only says the brand is "not responsible for lost or stolen packages".

If a question depends on any of these, the assistant must say the information is not in the knowledge base.

## Useful for copy

Many Glow Recipe products cost less than $40. Examples from the catalog: Strawberry BHA Pore-Smooth Blur Drops $32, Blueberry Bounce Gentle Cleanser $34, minis $10–21. Pairing a full-size product with a mini is an easy way to reach the free-shipping threshold, for example Blur Drops $32 plus the Watermelon Glow toner mini $15. Any free-shipping copy must state the "$40+" condition and "U.S. only".
