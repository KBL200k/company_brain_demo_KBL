# Creative learnings: paid social, Q3 2022 to Q1 2023

## How to read this

- **Channels:** Meta (Facebook and Instagram Reels) and TikTok. Metrics are 7-day click / 1-day view attribution.
- **Primary metric:** CPA (cost per purchase). **Secondary:** thumb-stop rate (3-second views ÷ impressions) and CTR.
- A result is a **winner** only if the gap in CPA is at least 10% with at least 100 purchases per arm. Otherwise it is **inconclusive**.
- "vs control" means the evergreen ad running for that product at the time.

## Cross-test learnings

1. **Lead with the praise theme that customers actually use.** The three biggest CPA wins (CT-01, CT-05, CT-08) each led with the product's #1 praise theme from research/insights-*.
2. **Address the top complaint in education, not acquisition.** Complaint-led creative (CT-02, CT-03, CT-07) did not lower CPA. CT-03 did cut negative comments, so it is worth running as an always-on education ad.
3. **Compliance failures were all hook-level.** All three incidents (CT-04, CT-06 Hook B, CT-10) came from hooks or offers, not body copy. Run the claim check on hooks first (sop/ad-claim-review).
4. **UGC beat studio** in 3 of 3 head-to-head tests. Real application footage also lets us show pilling or white cast honestly instead of claiming they don't happen.
