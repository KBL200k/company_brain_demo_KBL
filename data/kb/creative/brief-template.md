# Creative brief template

Use this template for ads, creator briefs and campaign concepts. Every factual line needs a source: a product doc, a research/insights-* doc, a review_id or a policy rule. If a section cannot be filled from the knowledge base, write **"Unknown, not in knowledge base"**. Do not guess.

---

**1. Product:** name, product ID, price (from product/<id>)

**2. Objective:** awareness / acquisition / retention / education

**3. Audience:** who it is for (skin type, concern). Cite the skin-type segment from research/insights-<id>, and note any segment flagged "insufficient sample".

**4. Customer insight:** one sentence, with the theme %, and 1-2 verbatim quotes with review_id.

**5. Key message:** one line in brand voice (brand/voice-guide).

**6. Reasons to believe:** ingredients from the product doc, and the review theme share. No clinical claims unless the claims register approved them (CP-06).

**7. What we learned before:** relevant test IDs from creative/learnings-2023, or "no prior test".

**8. Mandatories:**
- disclosures: CP-04 Sunburn Alert, CP-05 reapply, CP-10 #ad
- the rule IDs that apply from the brand/claims-policy quick lookup

**9. Do not say:** product-specific banned phrases from brand/claims-policy.

**10. Format & channel:** for example a 15-30 second Reels/TikTok UGC video, with the hook in the first 2 seconds.

**11. Success metric:** primary metric and the threshold for a winner (creative/learnings-2023 "How to read this").

**12. Open risks / unknowns:** data gaps that affect this brief.
