# Brand voice & copy guide

## Voice in one line

**A skincare-smart best friend:** juicy and joyful about fruit and glow, but honest and precise about what actives do.

## Four voice pillars

| Pillar | Means | Sounds like | Never sounds like |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Joyful** | Sensory, playful, fruit-forward | "a juicy hit of hydration", "dewy, bouncy skin" | childish, all caps, more than 2 emojis per line |
| **Smart** | Explain the active simply and name it | "PHA + BHA gently exfoliate to refine the look of pores" | jargon without explanation, pseudo-science |
| **Honest** | Set real expectations, admit trade-offs | "start 2-3 nights a week", "patch test first" | "miracle", "instant results", "works for everyone" |
| **Inclusive** | All skin types and tones, no shame | "for every skin type in the Glow Gang" | "fix your flaws", "bad skin", "problem skin" |

## Vocabulary

**Use:** glow, dewy, juicy, bouncy, plump-looking, refined-looking pores, smooth-looking texture, Glow Gang, Glow-Tip, fruit-powered, gentle.

**Avoid (style):** flawless, perfect, flaws, anti-aging (say "fine lines"), hack, cheap, "holy grail" in brand voice (fine inside a quoted review).

**Banned (compliance):** see brand/claims-policy. Examples: cure, treat, acne treatment, whitening, chemical-free, non-toxic, sunblock, waterproof, clinically proven (without a study ID).

## Talking about scent

Scent is one of the most-mentioned themes in reviews, and it divides people. Examples from research/insights-*:

- Watermelon Pink Juice Moisturizer: scent is mentioned in 58% of reviews and is "mixed" (P428819).
- Blueberry Bounce Cleanser: scent is mentioned in 51% of reviews and is "mixed" (P436359).

Rules:

- Describe the scent concretely ("fresh watermelon") but do not make it the only reason to buy.
- Never imply a fragranced product is fragrance-free or "for sensitive skin" (CP-09).

## Channel formats

| Channel | Length | Structure | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meta / TikTok ad primary text | 1-3 short lines | hook, benefit, proof, CTA | proof = real review quote with review_id (CP-07) or ingredient fact |
| Headline | ≤ 40 characters | benefit-led | no claims requiring REVIEW unless approved |
| Email subject | ≤ 45 characters | curiosity or benefit | max 1 emoji |
| PDP bullets | 3-5 bullets | what it is, key actives, who it's for, how to use | include CP-04 / CP-05 disclosures where relevant |
| Customer care reply | 3-6 sentences | empathize, answer, next step | see sop/review-response |

## Emoji palette

🍉 🍑 🥑 🍓 💧 ✨ 🌸. One per line maximum. Never use emojis in compliance disclosures.

## Examples

**Good:** "Glass-skin glow without the heavy feel 🍉 Niacinamide Dew Drops layer under or over makeup. 'This is my go to for making my skin glow' ★★★★★"
(The quote is verbatim from review/P466123-15736.)

**Bad:** "Clinically proven to erase dark spots in 7 days! The #1 serum for flawless skin."
(Breaks CP-03, CP-06 and CP-12, and the voice rule against "flawless".)
