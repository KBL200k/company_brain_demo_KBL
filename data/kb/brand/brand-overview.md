# Glow Recipe brand overview

This page summarizes how Glow Recipe presents itself publicly on glowrecipe.com (retrieved 2026-10-06). Internal tone rules are in brand/voice-guide. That document is synthetic.

## Positioning

- **Tagline:** "Clinically Effective. Fruit-Powered. Glowing Skin."
- **Ingredient philosophy:** "gentle, yet effective formulas using the highest quality ingredients". Each product pairs "antioxidant-rich fruit with gentle yet powerful actives".
- **Roots:** Korean skincare. The site has education hubs such as "Korean Skincare Routine 101" and a section on Korean skincare ingredients.
- **Education-led:** "At Glow Recipe, we want you to love & embrace your skin and that starts with learning about it!" The site's Glow 101 hub calls itself "your one-stop destination to learn about your skin".

## Product lines (from the catalog in this knowledge base)

This knowledge base covers the **fruit-powered skincare lines only**. Glow Recipe also sells a lip balm (Glow Lip Pop) and multi-product kits. Those were left out on purpose, so questions about them should be answered as "not in the knowledge base".

## Public commitments

- **Vegan** and **cruelty-free**. The ingredients page says "Vegan. Cruelty-free." and shows a cruelty-free logo. Sephora lists "Vegan" and "Cruelty-Free" in product highlights.
- Many products carry Sephora's **"Clean + Planet Positive"** highlight. This is Sephora's program label, not a Glow Recipe definition.
- Glow Recipe does **not** publish its own definition of "clean" or a list of excluded ingredients on the pages retrieved.

## Voice signals seen publicly

- Community names: "Glow Gang", "Glow-Tip".
- Friendly, simple descriptions of actives, for example "Super gentle exfoliating acids".
- Fruit and sensory language: juicy, dewy, glow.

## Not in the knowledge base

- Founding story, founders and company history. The pages retrieved do not cover them.
- Sustainability and packaging commitments. The site links to "Environmental Labeling", but the page was not retrieved.
- Clinical study results behind "Clinically Effective".
